NetOrchestrator

Safe network change management as small Go services.

Before a change touches a device, NetOrchestrator works out what it would cut off, requires the right number of independent approvals, and records every decision in a tamper-evident audit log. Source and design notes on GitHub.

The full workflow

The rest of the API (change requests, approvals, audit log) needs a token, so it is not open on this page. The repo's make demo-services runs it end to end: a critical change needs two reviewers, nobody can approve their own change, and the audit chain verifies afterwards.
